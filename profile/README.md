# KickThemBot

KickThemBot is a Telegram bot that sells access to private groups and channels: members pay into your own Stripe account or crypto wallet, get in, get reminded before access ends and are removed after it ends.

- **Website:** [kickthembot.com](https://www.kickthembot.com)
- **Start in Telegram:** [@KickThemBot](https://t.me/KickThemBot)
- **Pricing:** [kickthembot.com/pricing](https://www.kickthembot.com/pricing/). A free plan, and paid plans priced by active members, never by revenue.
- **For AI agents:** [one-prompt setup](https://www.kickthembot.com/agents/) and the [OpenAPI 3.1 contract](https://api.kickthembot.com/openapi.json)
- **For language models:** [llms.txt](https://www.kickthembot.com/llms.txt)
- **Security reports:** support@kickthembot.com ([security.txt](https://www.kickthembot.com/.well-known/security.txt))
